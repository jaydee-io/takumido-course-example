# Trainer & Apprentice

> The two roles in a TakumiDo live session.

::: slot left

## Trainer

The trainer **controls the session**: selecting a course, starting the session, and navigating through slides.

All connected apprentices receive slide changes in real time. The trainer sees a dashboard with navigation controls, a slide preview, and the list of connected apprentices.

The trainer can use keyboard shortcuts or on-screen controls to advance or go back. A dedicated projection window can be opened on a second screen for the audience.

:::

::: slot right

## Apprentice

The apprentice **follows the session** by joining via a simple URL and entering their first name — no account required.

Slides are synchronized automatically: the apprentice always sees the trainer's current slide in **Play** mode.

The apprentice can also browse previously presented slides freely in **Pause** mode, then resync to the trainer's position at any time.

:::
