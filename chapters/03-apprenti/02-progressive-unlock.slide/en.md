::: slot title

Progressive unlocking

:::

::: slot description

The ::accent[High-Water Mark] is the furthest slide the trainer has presented. It advances as they move forward, it never moves back, and it's broadcast to every apprentice at once.

- **Unlocked slides** — anything at or below the High-Water Mark: you can revisit them freely, as many times as you like.
- **Locked slides** — anything beyond it stays greyed out in the chapter menu: you can't run ahead of the way.

It's the Takumi spirit brought to the screen: you don't skip steps. You review what's been covered, you don't jump ahead to what's next. Mastery is earned in order.

:::
