::: slot headline

Resume without losing anything

:::

::: slot detail

A network drop — Wi-Fi cutting out, a phone going to sleep, switching networks — doesn't lose your place. TakumiDô preserves your ::accent[posture] across the disconnection.

On reconnection, everything is restored automatically, without a single action on your part:

- **Your posture is preserved** — if you were in ::badge[Play], you resume in Play; if you were in ::badge[Pause], you resume in Pause, on the same slide.
- **The High-Water Mark resyncs** — if the trainer advanced while you were away, the new slides unlock the moment you're back.
- **Your view realigns** — in Play, you return straight to the trainer's current slide.

A disconnection becomes nothing more than a brief interlude: the way waits for you right where you left it.

:::
